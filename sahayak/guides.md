## pccheck | How Sahayak spots files being locked on the computer
keywords: detect, detection, spot, catch, entropy, shannon, threshold, t1486, edr, heuristic, how detect ransomware, ransomware criteria, how do you know ransomware, signs, 2 of 3, two of three, decoy files, randomness, renames, cut off criteria, computer check
A program on the front-desk computer watches for 3 signs of files being locked.
The signs: our hidden decoy files are touched, files are rewritten as scrambled data, or many files are renamed in a burst.
If any 2 of the 3 happen within 60 seconds, the computer cuts itself off from everything except our server.
1. Decoy files: hidden files no real person opens. Touching one is a sign.
2. Scrambled data: at least 5 new files look random, scoring above 7.5 out of 8.
3. Rename burst: more than 20 files renamed in a minute, or 5 renamed to strange endings like .locked.
4. Two signs within 60 seconds: the computer is cut off at once, no tap needed.
5. Only the owner can reconnect it.
